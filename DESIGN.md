# Agavi AI — Design

Canonical rules for the bone-and-ink identity. Exported from Claude Design on 2026-08-27.

Tokens live in `tokens/`. Do not rewrite them. If a screen disagrees with the tokens, the tokens win. Do not invent a visual identity.

## Voice

Declarative. Plain. Short sentences. Often fragments.

Sentence case for headlines. Uppercase only for monospace labels.

No emoji.

## Wordmark

Set **Agavi.** in Newsreader. Ink for the letters. Terracotta for the period.

Do not invent a logo. Do not redraw the wordmark as an icon, ligature, or lockup that is not this type treatment.

Tagline, italic serif: *Operational intelligence — installed. A boutique AI consultancy for founder-led service businesses.*

## Marks

◊ opens kickers, meta lines, and lists. Not a bullet. Not a logo.

The agave motif is single-weight line art. Leaves radiate up from a center. No fill. Render it in Agave Deep on Bone or Ink. Use it as the counterpart to the wordmark — icons, splash screens, dividers. Do not invent a substitute plant, monogram, or mark.

## Editorial paper

Warm bone stock. Near-black ink. Deep agave green. One terracotta accent.

Never pure white. Never pure black.

Structure the page with hairline rules and whitespace. Not shadows. Not cards.

Corners are square. The only exception is pills — buttons, tags, badges — fully rounded (`--radius-pill`).

## Type

Newsreader for display, headings, and italic ledes.

Inter Tight for body, buttons, and UI.

JetBrains Mono for kickers, stamps, tags, and metadata.

Sizes, weights, tracking, and optical settings: `tokens/typography.css`. Do not invent a fourth family.

## Color

Named swatches and semantic aliases: `tokens/colors.css`.

Terracotta is the single accent. Agave Deep is the brand green. Do not add a second accent.

## Forbidden

No emoji. No blue. No gradients. No neon.

No dark cosmic theme. No Agavi Dispatch look.

No invented logo.
